What the terms mean

Key-to-key speed
The typical gap between two keystrokes, in milliseconds. Smaller is faster. It depends a lot on how you type and on your keyboard.
Typing rhythm variability
How uneven your pace was. A low value means an even, steady beat; a high value means bursts of fast typing split by stops.
Time spent paused
The share of your session spent in stops of half a second or more.
Revising
How often you went back to change something. Each run of deletions in a row counts as one revision.
Deleting
The share of your keystrokes that were Backspace or Delete.
Engagement reading
How much your pattern resembles emotional rather than neutral writing in the public sessions the model learned from.
