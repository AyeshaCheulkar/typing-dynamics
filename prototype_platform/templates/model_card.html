Model card

Honest documentation of the experimental effort model.

Model details

  • Task: estimate self-perceived writing effort (1–5) from typing behaviour.
  • Models: Random Forest (primary) & Linear Regression (baseline comparison).
  • Inputs: 9 behavioural features (speed, pauses, deletions, revisions, writing time, rhythm). The text is never used.
  • Status: Experimental research prototype.

Training data

  • {{ kpis.valid_sessions }} behaviourally-valid sessions from {{ kpis.participants }} participants (own study).
  • Self-rated effort labels; ratings concentrated at 2–3 (mean {{ kpis.mean_effort }}).
  • Participant identifiers are pseudonymous and kept local — not distributed.

Intended & out-of-scope use

  • Intended: research demonstration of an explainable writing-process pipeline.
  • Not for: assessment, grading, selection, diagnosis, or any decision about a person.
  • The estimate must always be shown as experimental, alongside (never replacing) the self-rating.

Limitations

  • Small sample (n={{ kpis.valid_sessions }}); results exploratory, not confirmatory.
  • Composed writing only; external transcription data differs in pause structure.
  • Self-report labels carry subjective variability.
  • Keyboard/device differences can affect timing.

Ethics & explainability

  • Every estimate is explained with SHAP (which behaviours moved it).
  • Recommendations are tied to observed behaviour, never to the predicted score.
  • Target = self-perceived writing effort. No claim about mental effort, stress, intelligence, emotion, or any psychological/medical state.
