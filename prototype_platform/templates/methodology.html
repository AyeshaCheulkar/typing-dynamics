How the study works

Methodology

From a writing session to an explainable, honestly-scoped estimate.

The pipeline

01
Capture

Every keystroke (down/up), timing and caret position are logged as the participant writes — the text itself is not modelled.

▸
02
Features

Keystrokes become behavioural features: speed, pauses, deletions, revisions, writing time and rhythm.

▸
03
Model

Linear Regression & Random Forest estimate self-rated effort, tested with leave-one-participant-out CV.

▸
04
Explain

SHAP shows which behaviours moved each estimate — describing the model, not proving causes.

▸
05
Report

An explainable report: measured behaviour, self-rating, experimental estimate and behaviour-tied suggestions.

Methods in brief

Evaluation

  • Participant-aware CV (leave-one-participant-out & GroupKFold) — the model is always tested on a person it never trained on.
  • Uncertainty — participant-bootstrap 95% CIs on every correlation and metric.
  • Honesty checks — a mean baseline, paired Wilcoxon vs baseline, and a permutation test for R².

Data & scope

  • Primary dataset: {{ kpis.valid_sessions }} valid sessions · {{ kpis.participants }} participants (own study).
  • External datasets (EmoSurv, KeyRecs) used only to validate the feature pipeline — never mixed with the effort labels.
  • Target = self-perceived writing effort; no claim about mental effort, stress, or any psychological state.

Extended analysis (same data)

Additional analyses that deepen the study without new data.

Task difficulty → effort

Moderate tasks were rated ~0.9 points higher in self-rated effort than Easy (participant-clustered model: +0.89, p = 0.063) — the strongest, nearly-significant result, and a face-validity check on the effort measure.

Deeper features & modelling

  • Where pauses fall: 57% of long pauses are between words, 12% within words, 1% between sentences.
  • Where edits happen: ~2 characters from the writing frontier — fixing recent typos, not revising far back.
  • Participant-aware model: pausing is the strongest behavioural predictor (+0.93 per SD, p=0.16).
  • All behaviour effects remain exploratory at n={{ kpis.valid_sessions }} — sample-size limited, not method-limited.
