Emotion-writing test

Write about a moment that mattered

You will write about a happy moment or a sad moment. We record how you type, and the tone of what you wrote, to study how writing about feelings relates to focus and typing behaviour. This is a research prototype — it is not a diagnosis or a measure of your mental health.

Before you start

Choose one prompt below and write for about 2–4 minutes. The page records your keystroke timing and analyses the emotional tone of your text. Afterwards you will rate how you felt and how much effort it took.
